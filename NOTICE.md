# Little Wins / 小小做到 — Attribution and modification notice

Little Wins is a substantially redesigned daily-achievement journal created by
the Little Wins contributors. Its product direction, information architecture,
interaction flows, visual system, Chinese copy, automatic achievement
classification, collection board, timeline, and annual review were introduced
as part of the Little Wins project in August 2026.

The project began from the open-source codebase of
[Nightlio](https://github.com/shirsakm/nightlio), created by Shirsak M. We are
grateful for that foundation. Portions of the authentication, Flask API,
SQLite persistence, deployment configuration, and earlier application modules
remain derived from Nightlio while the codebase is progressively refactored.
